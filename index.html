05

One script per format — say it before you’re hungry.

Format 01

Bowl bar

  • Grain or greens base
  • Double the protein
  • Load the vegetables
  • Salsa or light dressing

Skip: cheese, sour cream, chips, tortilla

Format 02

Salad

  • Grilled protein, never fried
  • Vinaigrette on the side
  • Add beans or avocado
  • Unsweet tea or water

Skip: creamy dressing, croutons, candied toppings

Format 03

Grill plate

  • Grilled chicken or fish
  • Two non-starchy sides
  • Sauce on the side
  • Water first

Skip: fries, bread, creamy sides

Format 04

Meal-prep pickup

  • Pre-portioned and labeled
  • Grab 3–5 at once
  • Confirm 25g+ protein
  • Store at eye level

Skip: deciding again later this week
